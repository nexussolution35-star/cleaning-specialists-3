Deep, End-Of-Lease & Move Cleans

The Detail A Regular Clean Doesn't Touch

A deep clean goes top-to-bottom into the places everyday cleaning skips — inside cupboards, ovens and drawers, behind and under movable appliances, along skirtings and door frames, into light fittings, switches and window tracks, and through the limescale that builds up in bathrooms. It's the reset that makes a tired home feel brand new again.

Our end-of-lease move-out cleans are built around what landlords and managing agents actually inspect, so your property hands back spotless and your deposit stays where it belongs — with you. We work to a room-by-room checklist and finish with a final walkthrough so nothing gets missed.

Moving in? Let us deep-clean an empty home before you unpack a single box, so your first night is in a fresh, sanitised, genuinely clean space. We're owner-managed, never a call centre, and everything we use is eco-friendly and safe around children and pets. You get a free on-site assessment and a written quote first.

What's included

  • Inside ovens, cupboards, drawers & wardrobes degreased and wiped out
  • Behind & under movable appliances — fridge, stove, washing machine
  • Skirtings, door frames, light fittings & switches detailed
  • Bathrooms descaled, with grout & tiles deep-cleaned and sanitised
  • Window interiors, tracks & sills cleared of grime and build-up
  • Full floor deep-clean and a final inspection walkthrough
