Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Home / Services / Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Carpets, Couches, Rugs & Mattresses

Lift Out The Dirt You Can't See

Carpets and soft furnishings hold far more than they show — ground-in grit, body oils, dust mites, allergens and the odours that build up over the years. A quick vacuum only skims the surface. We reach right down into the fibres to pull out what's actually living in your carpets, couches and rugs.

We work with two methods and pick the right one for each fabric: a low-moisture dry-clean that leaves carpets usable again within the hour, and a deep hot-water extraction for heavily soiled areas that need a proper flush. Both are safe for wool, synthetic and delicate fabrics, and both lift stains, allergens and odours rather than just masking them.

From lounge suites and armchairs to dining chairs, mattresses and loose rugs, we treat the lot — including dedicated pet stain and odour treatment. Ask us about stain protection on the way out, and your freshly cleaned carpets stay cleaner for longer.

What's included

  • Pre-inspection & fibre and colour-fastness test before we start
  • Pre-treatment of high-traffic lanes and individual spot stains
  • Low-moisture dry-clean or deep hot-water extraction, whichever suits the fabric
  • Upholstery, couches, armchairs & dining chairs refreshed
  • Deodorising plus optional stain-guard protection on request
  • Pile grooming and a fast-dry finish so rooms are usable again quickly
