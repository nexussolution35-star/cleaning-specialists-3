Disinfecting & Sanitising

Home / Services / Disinfecting & Sanitising

For Homes, Offices & Workplaces

A Genuinely Safe Space, Verified

When hygiene has to be more than visible, we disinfect and sanitise to a hospital standard. Using ULV fogging for fast, even coverage and manual treatment of every high-touch surface, we eliminate the bacteria and viruses that ordinary cleaning leaves behind — in homes, offices, schools, gyms, clinics and busy workplaces.

Our disinfectants are hospital-grade and SABS-approved, effective against a broad range of pathogens yet low-residue and eco-conscious. We target the points people actually touch — door handles, light switches, desks, keyboards and shared equipment — then issue a certificate of disinfection on completion so you can show your team, tenants or parents the work was done.

We're owner-managed, never a call centre. The same trained, police-cleared teams arrive on time, advise safe re-entry times, and offer rapid response after a confirmed illness or exposure on site. You get a free on-site assessment and a written quote before we begin.

What's included

  • Full-area ULV fogging for fast, even disinfectant coverage
  • Manual wipe-down of high-touch surfaces and contact points
  • Hospital-grade, SABS-approved disinfectants against bacteria & viruses

  • Certificate of disinfection issued on completion
