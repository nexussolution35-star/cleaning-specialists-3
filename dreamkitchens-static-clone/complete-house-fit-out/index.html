What is included in a complete house fit out?

A complete house fit out typically includes custom cabinetry, kitchens, wardrobes, vanities, storage solutions and bespoke joinery throughout the home. The goal is to create a cohesive interior that combines functionality, style and practical storage across multiple living spaces.

Can the entire house be customised to suit my design style?

Yes. Every fit out is tailored to your preferences, layout and lifestyle. Materials, finishes, cabinetry styles and storage solutions can all be customised to match your interior design and create a consistent look throughout your home.

What materials are used in a full house fit out?

We use high-quality materials such as polyurethane finishes, laminates, timber veneers, stone surfaces and premium hardware to ensure durability and a refined finish across all areas of your home.

How long does a complete house fit out take?

The timeframe depends on the size and complexity of the project. After consultation and design approval, manufacturing and installation are scheduled to ensure the project is completed efficiently and to a high standard.

Do you handle both design and installation?

Yes. Our team manages the entire process from consultation and design through to manufacturing and professional installation, ensuring a seamless and well-coordinated project.
