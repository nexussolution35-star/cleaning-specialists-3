Our Process

Creating a custom wardrobe requires careful planning, thoughtful design, and skilled craftsmanship. Our process ensures every stage is handled efficiently, allowing us to deliver wardrobes that maximise storage, enhance your space, and reflect the quality our team is known for.

1

Initial Consultation

We start by discussing your storage requirements, room layout, and design preferences to understand how your wardrobe should function within your space.
2

Concept Design & Planning

Our team develops a wardrobe concept tailored to your needs, planning internal configurations such as shelving, drawers, and hanging sections to optimise storage.
3

Transparent Scope & Quotation

A comprehensive quotation is prepared outlining materials, finishes, internal fittings, and project timelines so you have full clarity before moving forward.
4

Precision Measuring & Production

Once the design is confirmed, detailed site measurements are taken before your wardrobe is manufactured using quality materials and precise workmanship.
5

Professional Installation

Our experienced installers complete the wardrobe fit-out, ensuring everything is aligned, functional, and finished to a high standard.

Luxury Wardrobe Materials & Custom Finishes

Our custom wardrobes are crafted using premium materials and refined finishes designed to elevate both the style and functionality of your bedroom. We offer a wide selection of high-quality finishes including polyurethane, laminate, timber veneer, glass and custom joinery options, allowing each wardrobe to be tailored to suit your interior design and lifestyle needs.

What types of wardrobes do you design?

We design and install a range of custom wardrobes including built-in wardrobes, walk-in wardrobes, sliding door wardrobes and hinged door wardrobes. Each design is tailored to suit your space, storage needs and interior style.

Can my wardrobe be customised to suit my storage needs?

Yes. Our wardrobes are fully customised to maximise storage and organisation. We can include adjustable shelving, drawers, hanging systems, shoe storage, internal lighting and other accessories to suit your lifestyle.

What materials are used in your wardrobes?

We use high-quality materials including polyurethane finishes, laminates, timber veneers, glass panels and premium hardware fittings. This ensures durability, smooth operation and a refined finish.

How long does it take to design and install a wardrobe?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the wardrobe design. After consultation and design approval, manufacturing and installation are typically completed within a few weeks.

Do you design wardrobes for small bedrooms?

Yes. Custom wardrobes are ideal for maximising storage in smaller rooms. Our designs focus on efficient layouts, smart internal configurations and sliding door systems to make the most of available space.
