Our Process

We follow a clear, structured process designed to make your kitchen journey seamless and stress-free. From the initial consultation through to design, manufacturing, and installation, every stage is handled with precision, transparency, and care.

1

Consultation & Discovery

We begin by understanding your space, lifestyle needs, and kitchen design preferences to align your vision with practical, functional solutions.
2

Design Development & 3D Visualisation

Your kitchen concepts are developed into detailed layouts and realistic 3D renders, allowing you to refine cabinetry, finishes, and flow before production.
3

Transparent Quotation & Planning

A clear, itemised kitchen quotation is provided with full transparency on materials, scope, and project timelines.
4

Site Measure & Manufacturing

Precise kitchen measurements are confirmed before your custom kitchen is crafted using CNC-driven precision and expert local workmanship.
5

Installation & Handover

Our professional team completes your kitchen installation with care, followed by detailed final checks to ensure a flawless finish.

What kitchen services do you offer?

We design, manufacture, and install custom kitchens including cabinetry, benchtops, splashbacks, and storage solutions tailored to your space.

How does your kitchen design process work?

Our process includes consultation, 3D design visualisation, transparent quoting, precision measurement, custom manufacturing, and professional installation.

Can I see a 3D design before my kitchen is built?

Yes, we provide detailed 3D kitchen visuals so you can preview layout, finishes, and flow before production begins.

What materials are available for bench tops and cabinetry?

We offer premium options including Caesarstone, Corian, Granite, Timber Veneer, Polyurethane, Laminate, Vacuum-Sealed surfaces, and more.

Do you customise accessories and fittings?

Absolutely. You can choose from glass splashbacks, soft-close drawers and doors, premium handles, Hettich internal fittings, bi-fold and sliding doors, and more.
